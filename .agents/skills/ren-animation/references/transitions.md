# Transitions

Choose a transition from the relationship between the scenes and any
creative direction the project declares. There is no default transition
by mood, energy label or renderer convenience. If that choice is not
specified and materially changes the edit, ask rather than inventing it.

## Contract

A transition must preserve the timing and content the edit requests.
For an overlap, inspect both scenes at the shared time and verify that
neither becomes unintentionally blank or occluded. A cut is a valid
transition when it serves the edit.

## Mechanisms

The renderer may support cuts, cross-fades, pushes or other effects.
Discover its actual capabilities before choosing a mechanism; a named
effect is not evidence that the active renderer can execute it. Keep
creative intent separate from backend lowering.

## Verification

Use a contact sheet or frame samples around the handoff. Inspect for
blank, flashing, clipped or occluded content, and compare the result to
the intended relationship. Do not apply a universal transition length:
use declared timing or derive it from the requested pacing.
