## 3. What blocked the run, and what was done about it

### 3.1 A drawn transition at a cut whose outgoing picture is on V2 - **fixed by re-planning, no code change**

`compile_manifest` raised:

```
ValueError: Transition trans_009 at 38.801s does not sit at the end of any V1 clip
```

Both of the plan's `defocus` transitions were invalid. Every B-roll assignment, including
the ones filling `transition_slot` blocks, is placed on **V2** unconditionally, so V1 is not
continuous - it holds the 11 A-roll blocks with gaps where the slots are. A drawn transition
needs the cut to sit at the end of a **V1** clip, so **a cut whose outgoing block is a
transition_slot cannot carry one.** Five of this spine's fifteen cuts are in that class.

This is not bad luck. The handoff says "prefer placing major creative transitions on cuts
with a nearby beat", and in this spine the beat-aligned cuts are overwhelmingly the
`transition_slot-to-speech` ones - because a slot's duration is the only free variable when
snapping to a beat at 2.05, so the cut OUT of a slot is exactly where a beat can be put.
**Following the beat guidance steers directly at the cuts that cannot carry the effect.**

Fixed by moving both transitions to the other, V1-outgoing side of the same two seams
(cut 9 -> cut 8, cut 14 -> cut 13) and re-running `plan_transitions` and `plan_sfx`. The
`compile_manifest` assertion is correct and protective; the plan was wrong, not the code.

**Left for the captain:** `cuts_toon` already carries each cut's `type`, so it could carry
one more column saying whether the cut can take a drawn transition - or the handoff could
say the rule in a sentence. AGENTS.md section 5 states it; it just does not reach the step
that has to obey it.

### 3.2 Stabilization killed the render - **captain's ruling, minimal opt-in added**

The first `render` attempt built the whole timeline - 11 V1, 7 V2, 11 subtitle segments,
3 audio tracks, 17 Fusion comps, 2 transitions, 2 SFX, the OTIO mix - and then died on the
**first clip** of the stabilization pass:

```
── Neural Engine: 13 clips ──
  ✗ [V10] hook_hook: Stabilization
✗ FAILED (RuntimeError): Step failed (exit 1)
```

This is the AGENTS.md section 5 failure mode exactly: `neural_engine_directives` runs after
everything else is placed, so a build that dies inside it loses all of it.

**The captain ruled on 2026-08-26: "skip stabilization and move on."** The route taken is the
one section 5 prescribes - pop the directives off the **in-memory** manifest and leave the
file on disk carrying them.

**The one code change in this PR** is that opt-in, in
`library/steps/step_6_01_render/step.py`: an env var `PIPELINE_SKIP_STABILIZATION`, off by
default, which pops `neural_engine_directives` from the in-memory manifest immediately
before `build_timeline` and says on stderr that it did. The manifest in `pipeline_data.json`
and in `pipeline_output/steps/5_04_compile_manifest/output.json` still carries all **13**
directives, verified after the run: the plan is unchanged and only this run declined to
execute it.

**The consequence is real and should not be read as a formality:** this render is
un-stabilized. Nine of the eleven A-roll placements come from handheld selfie footage the
vision analysis labels `shaky`, and steadying was planned for 13 clips.

## 4. Found and left alone

None of these blocked the run. They are reported rather than fixed, per the brief.

### 4.1 A creative floor survives in the output validator

`plan_vfx` returned an empty plan - a deliberate answer, and one the handoff explicitly
permits ("an empty list is a legitimate answer for a piece that wants stillness"). The QA
loop **rejected it three times**:

```
QA failed on attempt 1, retrying: Validation failed:
- Step 'plan_vfx' output 'vfx_creative' is semantically empty: []
```

The floor has been removed from the prompt, removed from the post-bridge (whose comment says
so), and there is a test asserting it (`test_plan_vfx_accepts_an_empty_plan`). It survives in
the **generic** step-output validator, `validate_step_output` in `run_pipeline.py:1467`,
which flags any `required` list of length zero as "semantically empty". `vfx_creative` is
declared `required: true`, so an empty list can never pass.

It does not block - after the retries the runner proceeds with the best attempt and the empty
plan reaches the manifest intact. What it costs: three LLM round trips instead of one (320 s
against ~235 s for comparable steps), a "QA failed" line in the log of a step that produced
the documented correct answer, and - the real cost - **pressure toward the wrong answer**.
The retry appends the validation error to the context and asks the model to correct it. An
agent that treats QA feedback as authoritative will add a VFX it does not believe in on
attempt 2, and a padded plan looks exactly like a decision.

Minimal fix if wanted: a `may_be_empty: true` on that output spec, honoured by the `is_empty`
branch. One field, one condition, leaving the check intact everywhere else.

### 4.2 Two planning bridges are admitted stubs, and their tables carry nothing

**`plan_vfx/bridge.py`** says so in its own comment ("In a real implementation we would look
at semantic_analysis and timed_spine. Here we just pass an empty list or basic summary").
Its `vfx_candidates_toon` gives 11 rows in which:
- `text` is `slot.get("text","")[:50]` against A-roll rows that have no `text` key - always
  empty;
- `vfx_suggested` is the **hardcoded string `"No"`**, though the prompt describes it as
  "pre-computed... based on motion/pose data".

It also injects a hardcoded **`color_wash` at intensity 0.5** on the first segment. That type
is not in the step's own toolkit and nothing in `library/tools/fusion/` or
`library/tools/execution/` draws it. It never reaches the manifest (the post-bridge rebuilds
its output from `vfx_creative` alone) - but it reaches the **prompt**, where a pre-existing
coloured wash reads as a decision someone already made. That is a bridge stating taste, the
shape AGENTS.md 10.5 warns about.

**`plan_sfx/bridge.py`** produces a table with **zero rows**, on every project. It builds them
from `data.get("a_roll_assignments", {})`, and `a_roll_assignments` is **not a declared input
to that step** - the `.get` returns `{}` and the loop never runs. `action_sfx_suggested` is
likewise the hardcoded string `"No"`.

Both steps were planned from `timed_spine` structure instead. The one genuinely computed
field in either context, `available_sfx_types`, is correct and was used.

### 4.3 `cut_decisions` (step 3.03) has no reader

Searched repository-wide: the string appears once, in
`step_3_03_review_rough_cut/manifest.json`, declaring it as an output. The mechanical half of
that gate does gate the run through `rough_cut_review.passed`; the narrative half - checks 5
through 9, which the prompt itself calls "the most important step" - cannot fail the run
whatever it says. Worth naming in AGENTS.md 10.4's own terms: a gate that cannot fail reads
as coverage.

### 4.4 `stability` is `unknown` in two different projections

`broll_candidates_toon` (3.02) and `cuts_toon` (4.02) both report `Stability: unknown` for
every clip and on both sides of all fifteen cuts. Both prompts name it as a selection
criterion. **The value exists upstream** - at 2.01 the semantic documents give `shaky` for
clip_011 and `stable` for clip_017 - so it is being lost between the measurement and these
tables. Two projections, one missing field, same value present upstream: likely one defect.

### 4.5 Smaller things

- **`speech_sequence` duration bounds contradict its own handoff.** The prompt says "aim for
  ~75% of the target as your speech content duration"; 46.0 s of a 60 s target is 76.7%, and
  the validator warned `Total speech duration (46.0s) is out of bounds (54.0-66.0s)` - i.e.
  90-110% of target. One of the two is wrong.
- **The `clip_XXX` <-> filename join is never stated in any context.** The transcript says
  `clip_011`, the semantic documents say `IMG_1816_v3`, the catalog says `IMG_1816.MOV`. It
  was derived from row order and duration. Referencing a filename in `creative_direction`
  produced `⚠ WARNING: LLM returned unrecognized clip references`.
- **The transcript is in `speech_sequence`'s context twice** - as `transcript` (3 dp) and as
  `transcripts_toon` (2 dp), 110 rows each, in a 79 KB context.
- **`music_analysis` is ~74% of `plan_transitions`' 59 KB context** - the full 260-beat and
  65-downbeat grid in longhand, beside a `cuts_toon` that has already reduced it to one
  `beat_near_cut` column.
- **`plan_sfx` offers three SFX types the library does not have** (`foley`, `ambient` in the
  prompt; `reverse_cymbal` in the prompt and the schema). Only `available_sfx_types` reflects
  disk.
- **No creative brief reached any step**, including `plan_sfx`, whose manifest *declares*
  `creative_brief` as an input. Four handoffs devote a section to it. That is issue #214
  answered from the run's side: there is none in state.
- **`render`'s handoff contradicts itself on codec** - the Export Parameters table says H.264,
  the Implementation Notes say H.265. The file is H.264.

## 5. `validate` (6.02), and why it is not fixed here

```
Validation failed: 2 issue(s) found
  - [framing] picture occupies 100.0% of the frame (spread 0.31 over 119 samples) - the
    picture changes size within the video: 69.2% at 50.0s vs 100.0% at 0.0s
    (spread 0.31, bound 0.05) - one video has one geometry
  - [audio_levels] LUFS: -20.94, True Peak: -1.62 - integrated -20.94 LUFS is 6.94 dB off
    the -14.0 target (tolerance +/-1.0)
```

Both are the known failures the brief placed out of scope. Two observations that may be
useful anyway:

- **The framing failure has a structural cause this run surfaced.** All three clips carrying
  speech are true landscape (`rotation=0`) and are conformed into the portrait frame; all
  seven cutaway clips selected are `rotation=-90` and already display 1080x1920, so they fill
  natively. The run therefore alternates between conformed and native picture by
  construction. The sample at 50.0 s falls inside the climax block, which is A-roll. This was
  predicted before the render, in `review_rough_cut.md` section 4, from the rotation column.
- **True peak is fine** at -1.62 dBFS. It is the integrated level, not clipping, that fails.

## 6. Verification

Every claim in section 1 is a measurement of the delivered file
(`ffprobe`, `ffmpeg -af ebur128`, `blackdetect`), not a reading of the build log. Each
planning decision was traced through to the timeline:

- **The silent climax** - `_spine_blocks` carries `48.065-56.731 behavior=silent`, and the
  build reports `background_music: 24 keyframes, -96..-6dB` through OTIO.
- **The two SFX** - `sfx_001: TL 1038-1046 → A3` (34.6 s, block 8) and
  `sfx_002: TL 1384-1474 → A3` (46.13 s, block 13), at -18.0 dB and -14.0 dB.
- **The two transitions** - drawn as a tail on the outgoing V1 clip and a head on the
  incoming one, at both seams; 10 Fusion tools on those four clips against 8 elsewhere.
- **The empty VFX plan** - zero planned, zero added. The build log's "17 VFX" is the per-clip
  conform and house-look comps every clip receives, not planned effects.

One thing that could **not** be verified from the file: that the music is truly absent under
the climax. Per-block loudness off the render is the mixed signal and cannot separate music
from speech. The evidence is the manifest and the OTIO keyframe range, not the audio.
